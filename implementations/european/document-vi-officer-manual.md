# Document VI — The Officer Manual

## Duties, Limits, and Accountability of Chapter Offices

**Version 0.1 — Draft**

**Subordinate to Document I — The Charter, Document II — The Federation Protocol,
and Document III — The Local Bylaws Template.**

This Document elaborates the offices named in Document II, Article XII and
Document III, Article III into specific duties, qualifications, and limits. Where this
Document is silent, Document III governs election, term, and removal.

## Preamble

An office is a function the Chapter needs performed, entrusted temporarily to a
person accountable for performing it. It is not a rank, a title of honor, or a claim on
the Chapter’s loyalty beyond the work of the office itself.

This Manual exists so that no Chapter has to improvise, from scratch, what a
Treasurer or a Ritual Leader is actually supposed to do — and, just as importantly,
what they are not entitled to do by virtue of holding the office.

The rule stated in Document III bears repeating here, because every office in this
Manual is bound by it:

> **No officer holds authority beyond the specific function of their office. Ritual
> standing is not governing authority. Governing authority is not ritual standing.
> Neither is ever conferred by claimed revelation.**

## Article I — General Provisions Applicable to All Offices

**Section 1.1. Source of Authority.** Every office described in this Manual derives
its authority from election or appointment by the Assembly under Document III,
Article III — never from claimed lineage, claimed spiritual gift, founding status, or
self-declaration.

**Section 1.2. Scope.** An officer’s authority is limited to the duties listed under
their office in this Manual and any additional duties the Assembly formally assigns.
An officer acting outside this scope acts as an ordinary member, not with the
authority of their office.

**Section 1.3. Term and Renewal.** All offices are held for the term set in a
Chapter’s bylaws (Document III, Section 3.3), are renewable by re-election, and
carry no presumption of continuation beyond that term.

**Section 1.4. Accountability.** Every officer reports to the Assembly no less than
annually, or more often as the Assembly requires, and is subject to removal under
Document III, Article VI.

**Section 1.5. Combination and Vacancy.** Offices may be combined temporarily
to cover a vacancy, subject to the limits in Document III, Section 3.1
(Treasurer/Ritual Office separation) and Document VI, Section 9.1 (below). A
Chapter should not treat a temporary combination as a permanent structure without
Assembly action to formally restructure its offices.

**Section 1.6. Handoff.** An outgoing officer shall provide their successor, or the
Assembly if no successor is yet chosen, with all records, funds, keys, accounts, and
materials belonging to the office within a reasonable period, ordinarily no more than
**[HANDOFF PERIOD — e.g., 30 days]** of leaving office.

## Article II — Treasurer

**Section 2.1. Duties.** The Treasurer:

- holds, tracks, and accounts for all Chapter funds and material property;
- maintains financial records available to any member in good standing on
  reasonable request (Document III, Section 4.2);
- executes expenditures approved by the Assembly, and flags any expenditure
  request exceeding the threshold set in Document III, Section 2.2(a) for Assembly
  approval before acting;
- presents a financial report to the Assembly no less than annually.

**Section 2.2. Limits.** The Treasurer may not authorize expenditure above the
Assembly-set threshold without approval, commingle Chapter funds with personal
funds, or use Chapter property for personal benefit without Assembly authorization.

**Section 2.3. Qualification.** No formal qualification is required beyond Assembly
trust and demonstrated reliability; a Chapter may, but need not, require prior
bookkeeping experience.

## Article III — Ritual Office-Holders

**Section 3.1. General Note.** “Ritual Office” is a category, not a single role. A
Chapter may divide it among several people or combine several functions in one, as
it sees fit, subject to Document III, Section 3.1’s separation from the Treasurer.

### 3.2 — Ritual Leader

**Duties:** coordinates and typically leads the seasonal gatherings and rites required
under Document IV; ensures the structural minimums of Document IV (Article II’s
Acknowledgment/Threshold/Witness elements, Article III’s four seasonal gatherings)
are actually met.

**Limits:** may not require any member’s participation in a specific rite (Document
V, Article IV); may not use the role to override Assembly decisions (Section 1.2,
above; Document III, Section 3.5).

### 3.3 — Celebrant (Rites of Passage)

**Duties:** officiates or coordinates rites of passage under Document IV, Article II
(birth/naming, coming-of-age, partnership, death), which may be the same person as
the Ritual Leader or a distinct role.

**Limits:** as in Section 3.2; additionally bound by the consent standards of Document
V, Article IV, with particular attention to Section 4.3 (minors) where applicable.

### 3.4 — Historian / Storykeeper

**Duties:** maintains the Chapter’s Book of Names (Document IV, Article IV);
preserves the Chapter’s mythic, historical, and cultural material; leads or coordinates
the annual Remembrance Rite.

**Limits:** presents mythic material as cultural language, not required belief
(Document II, Article II); may not use claimed historical or genealogical authority to
exclude members on ancestry grounds (Charter Section 5.4).

### 3.5 — Land Steward

**Duties:** coordinates the Chapter’s ongoing relationship with its adopted place or
ecological feature (Document II, Section 5.1); organizes stewardship activity and
ties it to Offering practice where relevant (Document IV, Article V).

**Limits:** none beyond the general limits of Section 1.2.

## Article IV — Secretary

**Section 4.1. Duties.** Where a Chapter maintains this office, the Secretary:

- records and maintains minutes of Assembly meetings (Document III, Section 2.5);
- maintains membership records, including admissions, resignations, and removals;
- circulates notice of meetings, votes, and proposed bylaw amendments (Document
  III, Article VII) in accordance with required notice periods.

**Section 4.2. Limits.** The Secretary’s records are factual and administrative; the
Secretary has no independent authority to interpret or adjudicate disputed matters.

## Article V — Mediator

**Section 5.1. Duties.** Where a Chapter designates this office (or designates a
member ad hoc, per Document III, Section 6.1’s informal-resolution stage), the
Mediator facilitates the informal resolution and mediation stages of the dispute
process (Document III, Article VI, stages 1–2; Document VII).

**Section 5.2. Limits.** The Mediator has no power to impose a binding outcome;
mediation is facilitative, not adjudicative. Binding outcomes belong to the Assembly
(Document III, Section 2.2) or the Federation-level process (Document VII).

**Section 5.3. Recusal.** A Mediator with a personal stake in, or close relationship
to either party of, a given dispute shall recuse themselves, and the Assembly shall
designate a substitute or outside mediator.

## Article VI — Festival Coordinator

**Section 6.1. Duties.** Where distinct from the Ritual Leader, the Festival
Coordinator handles the logistical dimension of seasonal gatherings and rites of
passage: venue, scheduling, hospitality logistics (Document V, Article III), and
coordination with other Chapters or Distributions for joint events.

**Section 6.2. Limits.** As in Section 1.2; the Festival Coordinator’s logistical role
does not extend to ritual content, which remains with the Ritual Leader or Celebrant
unless the Chapter has combined the roles.

## Article VII — Membership Coordinator

**Section 7.1. Duties.** Where a Chapter designates this office, the Membership
Coordinator manages onboarding of new members and guests under Document III,
Article I, and serves as the primary point of contact for prospective members and
guests under the Hospitality standard (Document V, Article III).

**Section 7.2. Limits.** Admission and removal of members remains an Assembly
power (Document III, Section 2.2(b)); the Membership Coordinator administers, but
does not decide, membership status.

## Article VIII — Additional Offices

**Section 8.1.** A Chapter may create any additional office reasonably needed for
its operation (e.g., communications, archive/library keeper, youth coordinator,
interfaith or inter-Chapter liaison), subject to the general provisions of Article I.

**Section 8.2.** A newly created office should have its duties and limits documented
in the Chapter’s bylaws or an appendix to them, following the same duties/limits
structure used throughout this Manual.

## Article IX — Combination and Emergency Provisions

**Section 9.1.** The Treasurer and any Ritual Office may be combined in one person
only for a period not exceeding that set in Document III, Section 3.1, and only when
the Chapter’s size or circumstances make full separation genuinely impractical. The
Assembly shall review any such combination at its next meeting.

**Section 9.2.** In a genuine emergency (Document III, Section 6.2), any two
officers acting jointly may take interim action outside their ordinary scope to protect
member safety or Chapter property, subject to Assembly review within the period set
in Document III, Section 6.2. This is the only circumstance in which an officer may
act outside Section 1.2’s scope limit without prior Assembly approval.

## Article X — Relationship to Personal Authority

**Section 10.1.** No office described in this Manual — including the Ritual Office in
any of its forms — confers standing to claim the Chapter’s members owe the officer
personal loyalty, deference, or obedience beyond the specific duty being performed.

**Section 10.2.** An officer who begins to describe their office in terms of personal
authority, spiritual hierarchy over other members, or unquestionable judgment is
exhibiting the precise pattern Charter Article VII and Document III, Section 3.5 exist
to prevent, and is an appropriate subject of informal check-in under Document III,
Article VI, stage 1, well before matters escalate.
