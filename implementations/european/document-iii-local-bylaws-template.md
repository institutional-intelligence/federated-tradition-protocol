# Document III — Local Bylaws Template

## Model Governance Structure for a Chapter of the European Federated Tradition (EFT)

**Version 0.1 — Draft**

**Subordinate to Document I — The Charter and Document II — The Federation Protocol.**

A Chapter adopts this template, filling in bracketed fields, and may add provisions
that exceed these minimums but may not adopt provisions that fall below them or
contradict Documents I or II.

## Preamble

These are the bylaws of **[CHAPTER NAME]**, a Chapter of the **European Federated
Tradition (EFT)** operating under the **[DISTRIBUTION NAME]** Distribution,
located in **[LOCALITY]**.

These bylaws implement, at the Chapter level, the governance requirements of
Document II, Articles X and XII. Where these bylaws are silent, Document II and the
Charter govern. Where these bylaws conflict with Document II or the Charter,
Document II and the Charter govern.

## Article I — Membership

**Section 1.1. Categories.** A Chapter shall recognize at minimum two categories of
participant:

- **Member in good standing** — has completed whatever minimal onboarding the
  Chapter requires, holds voting rights in the Assembly, and is eligible for office.
- **Guest / Participant** — may attend gatherings under Document II Article VII
  (Hospitality) without voting rights or eligibility for office.

**Section 1.2. Admission.** A Chapter shall state its own criteria for admission to
membership, provided such criteria do not violate Charter Section 5.4 (no exclusion
by ancestry) or Charter Article III (no exclusion by metaphysical belief).

**Section 1.3. Right of Exit.** Any member may resign membership at any time,
effective immediately upon notice to any officer, without forfeiture of personal
property, and without retaliation, in accordance with Charter Section 5.3.

**Section 1.4. Good Standing.** A member’s good standing may be suspended only
through the process defined in Article VI of these bylaws (Discipline and Removal),
never by unilateral act of any single officer.

## Article II — The Assembly

**Section 2.1. Composition.** The Assembly consists of all members in good
standing. It is the highest governing authority of the Chapter.

**Section 2.2. Authority.** The Assembly holds exclusive authority over:

(a) the Chapter’s finances, including approval of any expenditure above
**[THRESHOLD AMOUNT]**;

(b) admission and removal of members;

(c) election and removal of officers;

(d) amendment of these bylaws;

(e) the decision to affiliate, disaffiliate, or change Distribution;

(f) any matter a member in good standing formally brings before it.

**Section 2.3. Meetings.** The Assembly shall meet no less than **[FREQUENCY —
e.g., quarterly]**. A quorum shall consist of **[QUORUM — e.g., one-third of
members in good standing, or a stated minimum number]**.

**Section 2.4. Voting.** Except where these bylaws specify a supermajority, decisions
of the Assembly require a simple majority of members present and voting. Each
member in good standing holds one vote. Proxy voting **[IS / IS NOT]** permitted.

**Section 2.5. Records.** Minutes of Assembly meetings, including votes taken, shall
be recorded and made available to all members in good standing.

**Section 2.6. Limits.** No Assembly decision may violate the Charter, Document II,
or the Compatibility Blacklist (Charter Article VI), regardless of the margin by which
it is approved. A decision in violation of these is void.

## Article III — Officers

**Section 3.1. Required Offices.** The Chapter shall maintain, at minimum, the
following distinct offices, per Document II Section 10.2:

- **Treasurer** — holds and accounts for Chapter finances and property; reports to
  the Assembly no less than annually.
- **Ritual Office-holder(s)** — as defined in Document II, Article XII (e.g., ritual
  leader, celebrant, land steward, historian).

A Chapter may hold multiple Ritual Offices or combine functions in one person,
except that the Ritual Office may not also serve as Treasurer for more than
**[EMERGENCY PERIOD — e.g., 90 days]** at a time.

**Section 3.2. Additional Offices.** A Chapter may create additional offices
(secretary, mediator, festival coordinator, membership coordinator, etc.) as it sees
fit.

**Section 3.3. Election.** Officers are elected by the Assembly for terms of **[TERM
LENGTH — e.g., one or two years]**, renewable by re-election. No office is
hereditary, self-declared, or transferable without Assembly approval, per Document
II Section 8.2.

**Section 3.4. Scope of Authority.** An officer holds only the authority necessary to
perform the specific function of their office. No officer may unilaterally bind the
Chapter financially beyond the threshold in Section 2.2(a), admit or remove a
member, or override an Assembly decision.

**Section 3.5. Explicit Limitation.** No officer, regardless of ritual role, claimed
spiritual insight, or founding status, may invoke supernatural authority, personal
revelation, or claimed ancestral sanction as grounds for exercising authority beyond
that granted under this Article, per Charter Article VII.

**Section 3.6. Removal.** An officer may be removed by the Assembly, before the
end of their term, through the process in Article VI of these bylaws.

## Article IV — Finances and Property

**Section 4.1.** All Chapter funds and property are held for the benefit of the Chapter
as a whole, not any individual member or officer.

**Section 4.2.** The Treasurer shall maintain records of all income and expenditure,
available for review by any member in good standing upon reasonable request.

**Section 4.3.** In the event of Chapter dissolution, remaining funds and property
shall be distributed as the Assembly determines by supermajority vote, consistent
with any applicable law and, where relevant, offered first to another Chapter or
Distribution of the Federation.

## Article V — Ritual and Calendar Practice

**Section 5.1.** The Chapter shall observe the seasonal minimum of Document II
Article IX (no fewer than four seasonal gatherings annually) and the ancestral
remembrance minimum of Document II Article IV (an annual reading of the
Chapter’s Book of Names).

**Section 5.2.** The specific ritual calendar, naming conventions, and practices of
the Chapter are set by **[the Ritual Office-holder(s), subject to Assembly approval /
the Assembly directly]** — Chapter to specify.

**Section 5.3.** Nothing in this Article requires any member to participate in a
specific rite that conflicts with their personal conscience; non-participation in a
specific rite does not affect good standing.

## Article VI — Discipline, Dispute, and Removal

**Section 6.1.** This Article implements Document VII (the Federation Dispute
Procedure) at the Chapter level. Any dispute, complaint, or proposal for discipline or
removal shall proceed through the following stages before resulting in loss of
membership or office:

1. **Informal resolution** — direct conversation between the parties, or with the
   assistance of the Mediator office (if one exists) or another member both parties
   trust.
2. **Mediation** — a structured process facilitated by a member or outside party
   agreed upon by both sides, if informal resolution fails.
3. **Assembly review** — if mediation fails or is refused, the matter is brought
   before the Assembly, which may take action including formal warning, suspension
   of good standing, or removal from office or membership, by the vote threshold in
   Section 6.2.
4. **Appeal** — a member subject to Assembly action under stage 3 may request
   one reconsideration by the Assembly, with new information or argument, within
   **[APPEAL WINDOW — e.g., 30 days]**.
5. **Separation** — if the matter remains unresolved or the underlying conflict is
   irreconcilable, the member or Chapter may exercise the Right of Exit (Article I,
   Section 1.3; Charter Section 5.3), or, at the Federation level, disaffiliation may
   proceed under Document VII.

**Section 6.2.** Removal of a member’s good standing or removal of an officer before
the end of their term requires a **[VOTE THRESHOLD — e.g., two-thirds]** vote of
the Assembly, following stages 1–3 above, except in cases of immediate safety risk,
which may be addressed by emergency interim suspension by **[e.g., any two
officers acting jointly]**, subject to Assembly review within **[e.g., 14 days]**.

**Section 6.3.** No disciplinary action under this Article may be based solely on a
member’s theological interpretation, protected under Charter Article III and
Document II Article II, or on their ancestry, protected under Charter Section 5.4.

## Article VII — Amendment of These Bylaws

**Section 7.1.** These bylaws may be amended by a **[VOTE THRESHOLD — e.g.,
two-thirds]** vote of the Assembly at a meeting for which the proposed amendment
was circulated to all members at least **[NOTICE PERIOD — e.g., 14 days]** in
advance.

**Section 7.2.** No amendment may reduce these bylaws below the minimums
established in Document II, or conflict with the Charter. Any such amendment is void
regardless of the vote by which it was approved.

## Article VIII — Affiliation

**Section 8.1.** This Chapter affirms the ten Foundational Principles of Charter
Article II and the Minimum Standards of Document II as a condition of its affiliation
with the **European Federated Tradition (EFT)**.

**Section 8.2.** A decision to disaffiliate from the Federation, or to change
Distribution, requires an Assembly vote under Section 2.2(e) and follows the notice
and process defined in Document VII.
