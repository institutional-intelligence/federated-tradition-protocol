# European Federated Tradition

**Reference Implementation of the Federated Tradition Protocol**

**Status:** Draft  
**Version:** 0.1

The **European Federated Tradition (EFT)** is the first reference implementation of the [Federated Tradition Protocol](../../federated-tradition-protocol-core-v0.1.md).

EFT applies the religion-neutral FTP architecture to a modern federated religious and cultural tradition constructed from institutional patterns attested across multiple pre-Christian European traditions.

It is not a claim that a single historical "European religion" existed.

Rather, EFT extracts compatible institutional patterns, adapts them to modern conditions, rejects incompatible historical practices, and deliberately constructs a new federated system.

## Relationship to FTP

FTP defines the institutional architecture.

EFT supplies a particular religious and cultural implementation of that architecture.

Accordingly, EFT adds substantive elements that are not required by the religion-neutral Core, including:

- household continuity
- ancestral remembrance
- sacred locality and stewardship
- sacred plurality
- reciprocity
- hospitality
- reputation and responsibility
- seasonal observance
- ritual practice
- mythic plurality
- offering

These are **EFT requirements**, not universal FTP requirements.

## Architecture

European Federated Tradition v0.1 is defined through seven governing documents:

1. **Charter** — purpose, foundational principles, rights, and compatibility boundaries
2. **Federation Protocol** — minimum standards shared across affiliated communities
3. **Local Bylaws Template** — governance of individual Chapters
4. **Ritual Framework** — rites of passage, seasonal observance, remembrance, and ritual structure
5. **Ethical Code** — reciprocity, hospitality, reputation, consent, and stewardship
6. **Officer Manual** — duties, limits, and accountability of institutional offices
7. **Dispute Procedure** — conflict resolution at Chapter and Federation levels

Together these documents constitute the EFT v0.1 governing architecture.

## Design Principle

EFT follows the FTP distinction between strongly specified institutional structure and locally adaptable traditional content.

Within EFT:

> **Practice is required. Belief is not. Structure is specified. Content is local.**

Different distributions and Chapters may therefore develop different mythologies, pantheons, ritual languages, calendars, cultural emphases, and metaphysical interpretations while remaining institutionally compatible.

## Status

EFT v0.1 is an experimental reference implementation.

Its purpose is both to establish a usable tradition and to demonstrate how the religion-neutral Federated Tradition Protocol can be instantiated as a concrete institutional system.
