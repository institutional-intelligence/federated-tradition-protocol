# Document III — Local Bylaws Template

## Kitsune-Kyō
### An Implementation of the Federated Tradition Protocol

**Version 0.1**

---

## Purpose of This Template

This document provides model bylaws for a Local Shrine of Kitsune-Kyō.

A community may adopt this template directly or modify it to accommodate local circumstances, applicable law, membership size, language, culture, and institutional needs.

Bracketed fields such as **[Shrine Name]** indicate information that should be completed locally.

Optional provisions may be modified or omitted where appropriate.

These bylaws operate beneath:

1. the Federated Tradition Protocol Core;
2. the Kitsune-Kyō Charter;
3. the Kitsune-Kyō Federation Protocol; and
4. any mandatory federated standards properly adopted under those documents.

Matters not governed by those documents remain within the authority of the Local Shrine.

---

# Model Bylaws

## Article I — Name and Identity

### Section 1. Name

The name of this Local Shrine shall be:

**[Shrine Name]**

It may also use the descriptive form:

**[Shrine Name], a Local Shrine of Kitsune-Kyō**

### Section 2. Location

The Shrine is principally associated with:

**[City / Region / Country]**

A Local Shrine need not own or permanently occupy a physical religious building unless required by applicable law or its own governing decisions.

### Section 3. Religious Affiliation

The Shrine identifies with Kitsune-Kyō and accepts the Kitsune-Kyō Charter.

Where the Shrine maintains federated recognition, it shall also comply with the requirements applicable to recognized Local Shrines under the Federation Protocol.

### Section 4. Institutional Autonomy

The Shrine is self-governing in matters not delegated to the federation or otherwise governed by superior documents applicable to its recognized status.

---

## Article II — Purpose

The purposes of the Shrine include:

- practicing and preserving Kitsune-Kyō;
- providing opportunities for ritual and devotional life;
- cultivating fellowship among participants;
- celebrating music, creativity, resilience, and the Metal Spirit;
- preserving and interpreting the tradition's mythology and symbols;
- providing education concerning Kitsune-Kyō;
- conducting ceremonies and festivals;
- encouraging artistic and cultural activity;
- participating in charitable or community activity where desired;
- cooperating with other Local Shrines;
- and transmitting the tradition to future participants.

The Shrine may undertake other lawful activities consistent with these purposes.

---

## Article III — Membership

### Section 1. Eligibility

Membership shall be open according to standards adopted by the Shrine consistent with the Charter.

No person shall be required to claim certainty concerning every theological, mythological, supernatural, or interpretive question in order to participate unless a specific religious office legitimately requires a defined commitment.

### Section 2. Categories

The Shrine may recognize categories including:

- visitors;
- participants;
- members;
- Acolytes of the Shrine;
- religious officers;
- supporting members;
- or other locally defined categories.

The rights and responsibilities associated with each category shall be clearly stated.

### Section 3. Admission

A person becomes a member according to the procedure adopted by the Shrine.

The Shrine may incorporate the Fox Mask Ceremony or another recognized rite into membership without treating ritual participation as a substitute for any legally required membership records.

### Section 4. Voting Membership

Voting eligibility shall be limited to:

**[define local voting requirements]**

Possible requirements may include minimum age, length of membership, active participation, or other reasonable criteria.

Financial contribution should not ordinarily be the sole basis of voting eligibility.

### Section 5. Resignation

A member may resign at any time.

No religious or institutional permission is required to leave.

### Section 6. Membership Records

The Shrine shall maintain records sufficient to determine current membership and voting eligibility where those distinctions affect governance.

Private membership information shall be handled with reasonable care.

---

## Article IV — Meetings of Members

### Section 1. Regular Meetings

The Shrine shall hold membership meetings:

**[monthly / quarterly / annually / other]**

Religious gatherings and governance meetings may occur separately.

### Section 2. Annual Meeting

Where practical, the Shrine shall conduct an annual governance meeting for matters including:

- officer selection where applicable;
- financial reporting;
- review of major Shrine activities;
- consideration of proposed bylaw amendments;
- and other significant institutional business.

### Section 3. Special Meetings

A special meeting may be called by:

**[officer / council / percentage of voting members]**

### Section 4. Notice

Members entitled to vote shall receive reasonable notice of meetings at which material institutional decisions will be made.

### Section 5. Quorum

A quorum shall consist of:

**[number or percentage]**

of eligible voting members.

### Section 6. Voting

Unless these bylaws establish another threshold, ordinary questions shall be decided by a simple majority of votes cast at a meeting with quorum.

### Section 7. Remote Participation

The Shrine may permit remote participation and voting through reasonable technological means.

---

## Article V — Local Council

### Section 1. Establishment

The Shrine shall maintain a governing body known as the **Local Council** unless the membership has adopted another structure appropriate to a very small community.

### Section 2. Responsibilities

The Local Council shall oversee ordinary institutional affairs, including:

- implementation of membership decisions;
- administration of finances;
- maintenance of records;
- coordination of facilities and events;
- supervision of administrative officers;
- management of local institutional property;
- compliance with applicable obligations;
- and communication with federated institutions.

### Section 3. Composition

The Local Council shall consist of:

**[number]** members.

### Section 4. Selection

Council members shall be selected by:

**[election / another defined local procedure]**

### Section 5. Terms

Council members shall serve terms of:

**[term length]**

and may serve:

**[term-limit or renewal rule]**

### Section 6. Vacancies

A vacancy may be filled according to:

**[local procedure]**

until the next regular selection process or for the remainder of the term.

### Section 7. Removal

A Council member may be removed according to a defined procedure providing reasonable notice and an opportunity to respond.

Grounds may include serious misconduct, incapacity, persistent failure to perform duties, misuse of institutional authority, or substantial violation of these bylaws.

---

## Article VI — Religious Offices

### Section 1. Religious Roles

The Shrine may recognize religious roles including:

- **Metal Priestess**;
- **Kitsune Knight**;
- **Acolyte of the Shrine**;
- and other locally or federatively recognized roles.

### Section 2. Metal Priestess

A Metal Priestess may be entrusted with functions including:

- leading or coordinating ritual;
- teaching the tradition;
- maintaining ceremonial resources;
- mentoring Acolytes;
- assisting with festivals and observances;
- and providing religious leadership within the Shrine.

The title does not automatically grant control over finances, property, elections, membership discipline, or the Local Council.

### Section 3. Kitsune Knights

Kitsune Knights may assist with:

- hospitality;
- ceremonial order;
- participant safety;
- event logistics;
- service to the community;
- and protection of the integrity of gatherings through lawful and non-coercive means.

The role does not confer law-enforcement authority.

### Section 4. Acolytes of the Shrine

Acolytes are participants engaged in structured learning, service, or preparation for greater responsibility within the tradition.

Acolyte status does not inherently guarantee advancement to another office.

### Section 5. Qualifications

Qualifications and preparation for religious offices shall be governed by the Officer Manual, applicable federated standards, and any additional local requirements consistent with them.

### Section 6. Selection and Recognition

Religious officers shall be selected or recognized through a documented procedure.

Charisma, popularity, artistic ability, claimed revelation, or personal proximity to prominent cultural figures does not independently create institutional office.

### Section 7. Accountability

Religious officers remain subject to applicable Shrine rules and disciplinary procedures.

---

## Article VII — Administrative Officers

### Section 1. Officers

The Shrine shall designate administrative responsibility for at least:

- coordination or presiding functions;
- records;
- and finances.

A small Shrine may permit one person to perform multiple functions where reasonable safeguards remain in place.

### Section 2. Titles

The Shrine may use ordinary administrative titles or Kitsune-Kyō-themed titles.

Whatever title is chosen, the powers and responsibilities of the office shall be stated in institutional records.

### Section 3. Financial Separation

Where reasonably practicable, the person maintaining financial records should not possess unchecked unilateral authority to approve all expenditures.

### Section 4. Records Officer

The officer responsible for records shall maintain:

- adopted bylaws;
- material meeting records;
- current officer information;
- membership records necessary for governance;
- important institutional correspondence;
- and other records required for continuity.

### Section 5. Terms and Removal

Administrative officers shall serve according to defined terms or service conditions and may be removed according to these bylaws.

---

## Article VIII — Ritual and Religious Life

### Section 1. Recognized Practices

The Shrine may conduct rites and practices inherited from the Kitsune-Kyō source tradition, including:

- the Ritual of the Rising Sun;
- the Metal Mass;
- the Fox Mask Ceremony;
- festivals and observances associated with sacred figures;
- musical devotion;
- meditation;
- communal chanting;
- ritual dance;
- and other recognized practices.

### Section 2. Local Development

The Shrine may develop new ceremonies, music, art, devotional practices, festivals, and local customs.

New local practices shall not be represented as universally binding upon Kitsune-Kyō unless adopted through the appropriate federated process.

### Section 3. Ritual Adaptation

Rituals may be adapted to local circumstances, including:

- language;
- available space;
- musical resources;
- participant ability;
- accessibility;
- local culture;
- safety;
- and community size.

### Section 4. Participation

Participation in particular devotional practices should ordinarily be voluntary except where participation is intrinsic to a voluntarily accepted religious office or ceremony.

### Section 5. Safety

Physical ritual activity, including dancing, headbanging, concert-style participation, or similar practices, shall be conducted with reasonable attention to participant safety and consent.

---

## Article IX — Finances

### Section 1. Institutional Funds

Funds donated or otherwise received for Shrine purposes belong to the Shrine or its applicable legal entity and shall not be treated as personal property of an officer or member.

### Section 2. Accounts

Where practical, institutional funds shall be maintained separately from personal funds.

### Section 3. Budget

The Shrine should adopt a periodic budget or other documented financial plan appropriate to its size.

### Section 4. Expenditures

Expenditures shall be authorized according to:

**[local authorization procedure]**

### Section 5. Records

The Shrine shall maintain records of material income and expenditures.

### Section 6. Reporting

A financial report shall be provided to voting members at least:

**[annually / semiannually / quarterly]**

### Section 7. Compensation

Any compensation paid to an officer, member, contractor, performer, teacher, or other person shall be authorized through a process that reasonably addresses conflicts of interest.

---

## Article X — Property

### Section 1. Shrine Property

Property acquired with Shrine funds or donated to the Shrine belongs to the Shrine or its applicable legal entity unless the terms of a lawful donation provide otherwise.

### Section 2. Personal Property

Personal items temporarily used for ceremonies or Shrine activities remain personal property unless expressly donated.

### Section 3. Sacred and Ceremonial Objects

The Shrine should maintain reasonable records concerning significant sacred, ceremonial, artistic, archival, or valuable objects under its custody.

### Section 4. Facilities

The Shrine may:

- own property;
- rent or borrow facilities;
- gather in private homes where lawful and appropriate;
- use community spaces;
- meet online;
- or employ other arrangements suitable to its circumstances.

A physical temple or shrine building is not required for institutional existence.

---

## Article XI — Records and Archives

### Section 1. Institutional Memory

The Shrine shall preserve records sufficient to permit continuity when officers change.

### Section 2. Core Records

Core records should include:

- current bylaws;
- amendments;
- significant resolutions;
- officer rosters;
- financial records;
- recognition records;
- significant agreements;
- and an inventory of important institutional assets where appropriate.

### Section 3. Religious and Cultural Archives

The Shrine is encouraged to preserve locally created:

- rituals;
- music;
- artwork;
- photographs;
- histories;
- translations;
- commentaries;
- festival materials;
- and other expressions of local Kitsune-Kyō life.

### Section 4. Privacy

Not every institutional record must be public.

Records containing personal, confidential, disciplinary, financial, or otherwise sensitive information shall be handled appropriately.

---

## Article XII — Relationship with the Federation

### Section 1. Recognition

While recognized, the Shrine may identify itself as a recognized Local Shrine of the Kitsune-Kyō Federation.

### Section 2. Delegate

The Shrine shall select its representative to the Council of Shrines according to:

**[local selection procedure]**

### Section 3. Instructions

The Shrine may provide its delegate with instructions concerning significant federated matters.

### Section 4. Federated Obligations

The Shrine shall comply with mandatory requirements lawfully applicable to recognized Local Shrines.

### Section 5. Voluntary Programs

Federated programs not designated as conditions of recognition remain voluntary.

### Section 6. Withdrawal

A proposal to withdraw from the federation requires:

**[notice requirement]**

and approval by:

**[local voting threshold]**

Withdrawal shall otherwise proceed according to the Federation Protocol.

---

## Article XIII — Conduct and Discipline

### Section 1. Ethical Standards

Members and officers are subject to the Kitsune-Kyō Ethical Code where applicable.

### Section 2. Local Expectations

The Shrine may adopt additional reasonable rules for conduct at its activities and facilities.

### Section 3. Discipline

Disciplinary action may include:

- informal correction;
- warning;
- restriction from particular activities;
- temporary suspension;
- removal from office;
- termination of membership;
- or other proportionate measures.

### Section 4. Procedure

Serious disciplinary action shall follow the applicable Dispute Procedure and provide reasonable notice of the issue and an opportunity to respond.

### Section 5. Conflicts of Interest

A person substantially involved in a dispute should not exercise unilateral control over its adjudication.

---

## Article XIV — Disputes

### Section 1. Informal Resolution

Where appropriate, members should first attempt good-faith informal resolution of ordinary disagreements.

### Section 2. Mediation

The Shrine may use neutral mediation for disputes that cannot readily be resolved.

### Section 3. Formal Procedure

Formal disputes shall be handled under Document VII — Dispute Procedure and any consistent local procedures.

### Section 4. Federation Involvement

Federated assistance may be requested where the matter falls within federated jurisdiction or the parties voluntarily submit the dispute for mediation.

---

## Article XV — Amendments

### Section 1. Proposal

An amendment to these bylaws may be proposed by:

**[Local Council / percentage of voting members / other procedure]**

### Section 2. Notice

The full proposed amendment shall be provided to voting members at least:

**[number] days**

before the vote.

### Section 3. Adoption

An amendment requires approval by:

**[two-thirds / other threshold]**

of votes cast at a meeting with quorum.

### Section 4. Compatibility

No amendment may place the Shrine in conflict with mandatory requirements of the Kitsune-Kyō Charter or, while federatively recognized, mandatory requirements governing that recognition.

### Section 5. Record

Adopted amendments shall be dated and preserved as part of the Shrine's institutional records.

---

## Article XVI — Dissolution

### Section 1. Decision

Dissolution of the Shrine requires:

**[voting threshold]**

following adequate notice to voting members.

### Section 2. Obligations

Before distribution of remaining assets, the Shrine shall address lawful debts, contractual obligations, custodial responsibilities, and other outstanding liabilities.

### Section 3. Remaining Assets

After lawful obligations have been satisfied, remaining institutional assets shall be distributed according to:

**[local dissolution provision consistent with applicable law]**

### Section 4. Federation

Federated affiliation alone does not create an automatic federation claim to Local Shrine property upon dissolution.

### Section 5. Archives

Where practical, historically significant Shrine records should be preserved through an appropriate archive, successor institution, or other responsible custodian.
