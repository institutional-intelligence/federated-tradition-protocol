# 日本語版 — Japanese Translation

This directory contains the Japanese-language edition of **Kitsune-Kyō (キツネ教)**.

The Japanese edition is a localized translation of the canonical source material. It is intended to read naturally for Japanese readers while preserving the mythology, institutional structure, terminology, and overall character of the original work.

## Status

**Japanese Translation — v0.1**

The English-language implementation remains the canonical source for structural development of Kitsune-Kyō.

The Japanese edition is a maintained localization of that source rather than an independent fork of the tradition.

## Translation Principles

Japanese translations should prioritize:

1. **Meaning over literalism**  
   Translate concepts naturally rather than reproducing English syntax word-for-word.

2. **Consistent terminology**  
   Established Japanese terms should remain consistent throughout the translation.

3. **BABYMETAL terminology**  
   Where BABYMETAL has established official terminology, names, or Japanese song titles, those forms should be preferred.

4. **Appropriate religious vocabulary**  
   Japanese religious and ritual terminology may be used where it accurately conveys the intended function of the source material.

5. **Preservation of tone**  
   Mythological, ceremonial, prophetic, administrative, and explanatory passages should retain their distinct registers.

6. **No unnecessary romanization**  
   Japanese-language documents should normally use Japanese script and established BABYMETAL spellings rather than adding romaji readings for Japanese terms.

7. **Structural fidelity**  
   Translation should preserve the institutional meaning and functional relationships defined by the source documents, even where wording is localized.

## Established Terminology

The following forms are preferred throughout the Japanese edition:

- Kitsune-Kyō — **キツネ教**
- Fox God — **キツネ様**
- Metal Resistance — **メタル・レジスタンス**
- shrine / sacred worship space — **社**
- shrine maiden / priestess — **巫女**
- headbanging — **ヘドバン**
- silent vigil — **夜籠り**
- cycle of rebirth — **輪廻**

BABYMETAL member names should retain their official forms:

- **SU-METAL**
- **MOAMETAL**
- **YUIMETAL**
- **MOMOMETAL**

Official Japanese song titles should be used where available.

## Editorial Style

The principal prose style is dignified **である調**.

Prophecies, invocations, sacred sayings, and other explicitly mythological passages may use a somewhat older or more literary register where appropriate, while remaining intelligible to contemporary Japanese readers.

The objective is not to imitate archaic Japanese scripture literally, but to create a coherent ceremonial and mythological voice.

## Canonical Relationship

Translation does not create a separate canonical tradition.

When the Japanese translation and canonical source differ because of translation choices, the canonical source governs the underlying institutional meaning.

Translation-specific wording may nevertheless be improved independently when doing so produces clearer or more natural Japanese without changing that meaning.

## Corrections

Obvious linguistic, typographical, or translation errors may be corrected in the Japanese edition without altering the canonical source.

Substantive changes to doctrine, governance, institutional structure, mythology, or protocol should first be made in the canonical source and then propagated to the translation.

## Contributions

Contributions that improve the accuracy, consistency, readability, or cultural naturalness of the Japanese edition are welcome.

Proposed changes should preserve the distinction between:

- **translation/localization changes**, and
- **substantive changes to Kitsune-Kyō itself**.

Substantive changes belong upstream in the canonical implementation.
